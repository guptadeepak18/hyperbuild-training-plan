# Module 7: Use AI to Create a Business Plan

**⏱ Estimated Time:** 4-5 hours  
**Week:** 3 | **Focus:** Business & Knowledge Systems

---

## Learning Objectives

- Use AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity) to draft a full business plan
- Structure a plan: Executive Summary, Market Analysis, Strategy, Financials
- Learn iterative AI prompting for long-form business documents

---

## Why Use AI for Business Planning?

Traditional business plans take weeks of research, writing, and revision. With AI tools, you can:

- **Research markets** in minutes instead of days (Perplexity)
- **Draft sections** with structured prompts (Claude/ChatGPT)
- **Generate financial projections** with realistic assumptions
- **Iterate rapidly** by asking AI to critique and improve its own output
- **Produce professional-quality** documents ready for investors or stakeholders

The key is knowing how to prompt effectively and how to combine multiple AI tools for best results.

- Completed Module 3 (Perplexity) for research skills

## Business Plan Structure

Your final plan should include these sections:

| Section | Purpose | Pages |
|---------|---------|-------|
| **Executive Summary** | High-level overview of the entire plan | 1 |
| **Problem Statement** | The pain point you are solving | 1 |
| **Solution** | Your product/service and how it works | 1-2 |
| **Target Market** | Who your customers are and market size | 1-2 |
| **Competitive Analysis** | Who else is solving this and your advantage | 1-2 |
| **Business Model** | How you make money | 1 |
| **Go-to-Market Strategy** | How you acquire customers | 1-2 |
| **Team** | Who is building this (real or planned) | 0.5 |
| **Financial Projections** | 3-year revenue, costs, and profitability | 1-2 |
| **Funding Ask** | How much you need and what you will do with it | 0.5 |

**Total: 10-14 pages**

## Step-by-Step Activities

### Activity 1: Choose Your Idea and Research (1 hour)

**Step 1: Pick a startup idea.** If you do not have one, choose from these:

| Idea | Description |
|------|-------------|
| AI Study Buddy | Personalized AI tutor for college students |
| Local Service Marketplace | AI-powered platform connecting local service providers |
| Content Repurposer | Tool that turns blog posts into social media, newsletters, and videos |
| AI Meeting Assistant | Records, transcribes, summarizes, and assigns action items from meetings |
| Health Data Dashboard | AI-powered personal health analytics from wearable data |

**Step 2: Research with Perplexity.** Run these queries:

```
What is the current market size for [your industry]?
What are the growth projections for the next 5 years?
```

```
Who are the top 5 competitors in [your space]?
What are their strengths, weaknesses, pricing, and funding?
```

```
What are the main customer pain points in [your market]?
What do users complain about with existing solutions?
```

```
What are the typical customer acquisition costs and
lifetime values in [your industry]?
```

Save all Perplexity research outputs for use in later sections.

### Activity 2: Draft Each Section with AI (2-3 hours)

Use Claude or ChatGPT for each section. The key is **structured prompting**.

**Section 1: Executive Summary (write this LAST but put it first)**

```
Based on the following business plan details, write a compelling
1-page executive summary for investors:

Company: [name]
Problem: [1 sentence]
Solution: [1 sentence]
Market Size: [from Perplexity research]
Revenue Model: [how you make money]
Traction: [any early results or planned milestones]
Funding Ask: [amount needed]

Tone: Confident, concise, data-driven.
Format: 4 paragraphs - Problem/Opportunity, Solution, Market,
The Ask.
```

**Section 2: Problem Statement**

```
I am building [your product] for [target audience].

Write a compelling Problem Statement section for my business plan.
Include:
1. The core pain point (with real-world examples)
2. How people currently solve this problem (status quo)
3. Why current solutions fall short
4. The cost of NOT solving this problem (time, money, frustration)
5. A brief data point or statistic that validates the problem

Tone: Empathetic but urgent. Make the reader feel the pain.
Length: 1 page.
```

**Section 3: Solution**

```
My solution is [describe your product/service in 2-3 sentences].

Write the Solution section of my business plan. Include:
1. What the product does (clear, non-technical explanation)
2. How it works (3-step user journey)
3. Key features (top 5, with 1-sentence descriptions)
4. What makes it different from competitors
5. Technology stack overview (brief)

Include a "before and after" comparison showing life without
vs. with our product.
Length: 1-2 pages.
```

**Section 4: Target Market**

```
Using this market research data:
[Paste your Perplexity research here]

Write the Target Market section. Include:
1. Total Addressable Market (TAM)
2. Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
3. Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) - realistic year 1
4. Customer personas (2-3 detailed profiles)
5. Market trends supporting growth

Use real numbers where possible. Cite sources.
Length: 1-2 pages.
```

**Section 5: Competitive Analysis**

```
Here are the top competitors in my space:
[Paste Perplexity competitor research]

Create a Competitive Analysis section with:
1. Competitor overview table (name, founded, funding, users, pricing)
2. Feature comparison matrix (us vs. top 3 competitors)
3. Our competitive advantages (3 key differentiators)
4. Potential competitive threats and how we will address them
5. Our moat (what makes us hard to copy)

Be honest about competitor strengths. Investors respect honesty.
Length: 1-2 pages.
```

**Section 6: Business Model**

```
Write the Business Model section for [your product].

Our revenue streams are: [list them]
Our pricing is: [describe tiers if applicable]

Include:
1. Revenue model explanation
2. Pricing strategy with justification
3. Unit economics (CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC ratio)
4. Path to profitability
5. Expansion revenue opportunities

Use a pricing table if applicable.
Length: 1 page.
```

**Section 7: Go-to-Market Strategy**

```
Write a Go-to-Market Strategy for [your product] targeting
[your audience].

Budget: [your estimated marketing budget]
Timeline: First 12 months

Include:
1. Launch strategy (pre-launch, launch day, post-launch)
2. Customer acquisition channels (ranked by priority)
3. Content marketing plan
4. Partnership opportunities
5. Key metrics and milestones for months 1, 3, 6, and 12

Be specific with tactics, not just strategies.
Length: 1-2 pages.
```

**Section 8: Financial Projections**

```
Create 3-year financial projections for [your product].

Assumptions:
- Starting users: [number]
- Monthly growth rate: [percentage]
- Average revenue per user: [amount]
- Major cost categories: [list them]
- Team size by year: Year 1: [n], Year 2: [n], Year 3: [n]

Include:
1. Revenue projections table (monthly for Y1, quarterly for Y2-Y3)
2. Cost breakdown (fixed vs. variable)
3. Profit/loss summary
4. Key financial metrics (burn rate, runway, break-even point)
5. Assumptions clearly stated

Format as clean tables with clear labels.
Length: 1-2 pages.
```

### Activity 3: AI-Powered Critique and Revision (1 hour)

This is where AI really shines. Ask it to critique its own work:

**Round 1: Investor Perspective**
```
You are a skeptical venture capital investor reviewing this
business plan. Identify the top 5 weaknesses, gaps, or
unrealistic assumptions. Be brutally honest.

[Paste your draft]
```

**Round 2: Fix the Weaknesses**
```
Based on your critique above, rewrite the weak sections
to address each concern. Add data, strengthen arguments,
and make the plan more credible.
```

**Round 3: Final Polish**
```
Review this complete business plan for:
1. Consistency (do numbers match across sections?)
2. Clarity (can a non-expert understand it?)
3. Persuasiveness (would you invest?)
4. Professional tone and formatting

Make final improvements and output the polished version.
```

## Multi-Tool Workflow

For best results, use each AI tool for what it does best:

| Tool | Best For |
|------|---------|
| **Perplexity** | Market research, competitor data, statistics, citations |
| **Claude** | Long-form writing, structured sections, financial tables, critique |
| **ChatGPT** | Creative brainstorming, naming, taglines, pitch deck text |
| **Cowork** | Compiling all sections into a final formatted document |

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Accepting first drafts:** Always iterate at least 2-3 times
2. **Fake numbers:** AI may hallucinate statistics. Verify with Perplexity
3. **Too optimistic:** Ask AI to add realistic challenges and risks
4. **No differentiation:** Make sure your "why us" is crystal clear
5. **Wall of text:** Use tables, bullets, and headers for readability
6. **Skipping the critique step:** The self-critique loop is the most valuable part
